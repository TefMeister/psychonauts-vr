Then the pass did what that verdict made possible: read Astralathe's source through GitLab's REST API. **Five of its published offsets land exactly on the dossier's inferences** (player pointer `+0x818C`, `IsKeyJustPressed`, camera position `+0x08`, the `lua_dobuffer` shape, the matrix layout), it fixes the **`lua_State` at a static offset** off the same singleton, names the camera's own **`BoxVisible`** test by signature, and exposes `CallFunctionf` — call any Lua binding by name. Pointer dropped in `engine-research/inbox/`. Not answered publicly: the `+0xB8` parent, the bone record, the unit scale, a first-person camera API.
